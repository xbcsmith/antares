# Antares RPG — World & Storyline

## Campaign Title

### **The Stillness Prophecy**
*A Cosmic Weave Chronicle*

The title plays on the Lich King Eonir's obsession with freezing a single perfect moment in time forever, and on the prophetic visions (Zhaya's Astrologer's Path, Jyeshtha's timeline-sight) that drive the party toward him.

---

## The World

### **Velmoria**
Velmoria is not simply a planet — it is a knot in the **Cosmic Weave**, the unseen fabric that binds every world, plane, and possibility together. For millennia the Weave has been fraying at its edges, and wherever it thins, reality grows strange: mountains crack open, shadows learn to hunger, light itself can be gathered like water. The **Great Tremor**, the **Great Gloom**, and the **Great Alignment** — the very cataclysms that shaped Gareth, Mira, and Zara — are not unrelated disasters. They are wounds in the same fabric. Scattered across Velmoria are unstable tears in that fabric known as **astral portals**, which travelers (and worse things) use to cross vast distances in a single step.

---

## The Continents

### **Ashvale**
The temperate heartland continent and the party's starting ground. Rolling farmland gives way to the Dark Forest, the shattered stone teeth of the Ironpeak Mountains, and the smoldering slopes of Mount Ashkarron. Most of Velmoria's remaining free settlements cling to Ashvale's river valleys, including the town where the party's journey begins.

### **Khareth**
A continent scoured down to bone and glass by an astral cataclysm so old its name has been lost. Little grows here; what remains are sunken ruins, wandering nomads, and the Glass Sea — where the Astronomer's Temple has stood watch since before recorded history.

### **Vaelgrim**
The northern reach of the world, locked in permanent winter since Eonir claimed the relic and began "stilling" the land around his throne. Snow falls without wind. Rivers freeze mid-current. At its heart rise the Frostspire Peaks, and within them, the Lich King's crumbling seat of power.

---

## The Mountains

### **Ironpeak Mountains** *(Ashvale)*
The shattered range that once cradled Aethelgard, the dwarven city Gareth Deepstone gave his back to save. Half the range still bears the fracture line of the Great Tremor — a jagged scar visible for miles, and a place Gareth cannot look upon without remembering.

### **Aurelian Heights** *(eastern Ashvale)*
A serene, high-altitude range of monasteries and prayer terraces overlooking the clouds. Zhaya trained here for years before her visions of the Astrologer's Path drove her to abandon its discipline for the road east.

### **Frostspire Peaks** *(Vaelgrim)*
Jagged, ice-armored mountains at the northern edge of the known world. Nothing grows on their slopes and nothing that dies there decays — a symptom, locals whisper, of the stillness spreading outward from the castle at their base.

---

## The Volcano

### **Mount Ashkarron** *(Ashvale)*
A restless volcano whose smoke is said to trace the shape of old constellations across the sky before the wind tears it apart. Arcturus keeps his cave in its foothills — not for the warmth, he claims, but because "a mountain that still remembers how to change its shape is good company."

---

## The Desert & Pyramid

### **The Glass Sea** *(Khareth)*
A vast desert of dunes fused to glittering black glass, scorched flat by whatever cataclysm first tore Khareth open. At its center rises a single structure that the sands have never managed to bury:

### **Astronomer's Temple**
A great stone pyramid rising from the Glass Sea, precisely aligned so its capstone catches starlight from a hundred worlds at once. Jyeshtha has kept his vigil here since before the Glass Sea had a name, watching timelines the way other men watch weather.

# The Storyline

## Act I — The Gathering Storm

The adventure begins in the **Town Square**, where Kira has spent weeks quietly recruiting anyone willing to earn a little coin and see a little trouble. Gareth, Isolde, Mira, Whisper, Sirius, Zara, and Zhaya each arrive in town for their own reasons and are drawn into Kira's makeshift company. The **Village Elder** sets them to a simple task: clear a goblin incursion menacing the outskirts.

The trail leads into the **Dark Forest**, where "simple goblins" turn out to be scouts for something larger stirring in the deep wood. Following wounded survivors and half-burnt banners back through the trees, the party stumbles onto a hut that shouldn't exist — and the strange, half-lucid wizard living in it.

## Act II — Brothers of the Weave

That wizard is **Aetheris**, the Void-Drifter, who mistakes the party for old friends from a life he can't quite remember and just as quickly apologizes for it. Sensing something familiar — and troubling — in the goblin activity, Aetheris leads the party to **Arcturus's Cave**, at the foot of **Mount Ashkarron**, to consult the only person stranger than himself: his own severed other half.

**Arcturus** is amused, then serious. He confirms what Aetheris suspected: the goblin unrest, the storms over the Ironpeak Mountains, and a string of missing arcane instruments are not coincidence — the Weave is fraying faster than it has in centuries. Before he'll help further, Arcturus sends the party on errands any forgetful wizard eventually needs done: recovering a stolen astrolabe from the **Ancient Ruins** (crawling with kobolds and goblins using it for reasons unknown), and retrieving a sealed reliquary from the **Harrow Downs**, where the tombs of an advanced ancient civilization have started opening from the inside.

Passing through the **Mountain Pass**, the party rests and resupplies in the hidden village there, gathering rumors of a "stillness" spreading somewhere in the far north.

## Act III — The Astronomer's Warning
With his instruments returned, Arcturus finally opens an astral portal south — not to send the party home, but to send them to someone who has been waiting far longer than he has. They step through the portal into the **Glass Sea** and stand before the **Astronomer's Temple**.

Inside, **Jyeshtha** greets them like guests he has already met, in a future he's already seen. He explains the truth: for decades he searched for the **jade icosahedron**, a relic forged by the first astronomers to see across worlds and through time — the only tool capable of tracking exactly where and how fast the Weave is unraveling. He found it. And then **Eonir**, the Lich King, took it from him.

Eonir doesn't want to destroy Velmoria — he wants to *stop* it, to freeze one perfect, unchanging moment before the fraying Weave takes everything from him a second time, as it once took his mortal kingdom. But a relic built to observe cannot survive being used to arrest all motion; every day Eonir holds it, the stillness bleeds further outward from Vaelgrim, and the fraying elsewhere only accelerates. Jyeshtha cannot go retrieve it himself — his sight anchors him here, forever watching, never walking. He asks the party to do what he cannot.

Along the way, Zhaya recognizes the temple's carved star-charts from her own visions and finally understands her Astrologer's Path was never a person to find, but a road — the very one she's now standing on.

## Act IV — Into the Frostspire

Guided by an astral portal keyed to the Astronomer's own sight, the party crosses into **Vaelgrim**. The land grows quieter with every mile — no wind, no birdsong, snow falling in perfect silence — until they reach the **Frostspire Peaks** and the crumbling castle at their base: the **Lich King's Lair**.

Inside, they find not a monster but a mourning king, Eonir Xavian Stalix, still turning the jade icosahedron over in hands that no longer feel the cold. He speaks slowly, almost gently, of geometry and stillness and the terror of watching everything he loved fall apart a second time. He will not simply hand the relic over — some part of him still believes stillness is mercy — and the party must either convince him or take it from him by force.

Whatever the path, the confrontation ends with the relic finally free of Eonir's grip, and the Lich King released — one way or another — from the moment he'd spent so long trying to hold onto forever.

## Act V — Restoring the Weave

The party carries the jade icosahedron back through the portals to the **Astronomer's Temple**. As Jyeshtha finally holds the relic he spent a lifetime seeking, he turns its sight outward instead of inward — mapping every tear in the Weave at once, Ashvale to Khareth to Vaelgrim, and beginning the slow work of guiding it back together.

The stillness in the north begins to thaw. The tremors beneath Ironpeak quiet. The Great Gloom that Mira has fought her whole life recedes, if only slightly — a first sign, not a final victory, that a world can heal the same way it broke: gradually, and together.

Kira's makeshift company — thief and princess, scholar and cleric, dwarf and monk and mage — finds itself no longer a band of strangers who answered a call in a town square, but the first travelers Velmoria has had in generations who walked between its wounds on purpose, and closed them.

*The Weave holds. For now. And somewhere out past the stars Jyeshtha watches, other tears are still waiting to be found.*
