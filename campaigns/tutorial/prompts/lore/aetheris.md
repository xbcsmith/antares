# Aetheris Eldridge (The Void-Drifter)

## Backstory

He is a peculiar existence: an incarnation of the Great Wizard Arcturus himself, separated from his primary self when he accidentally stepped through a jagged rip in the Weave during a deep exploration of the Void. While Arcturus became a mentor and guide, this "Brother" remains a bit more displaced, as if still trying to shake off the lingering influence of the rift.

He possesses many of Arcturus's qualities but often feels slightly more eccentric—his magic is occasionally unpredictable, and his memories are colored by the strange, lonely colors of the Void. He carries a sense of longing for "home," even though his home is a place that exists between dimensions. He speaks with a humble sincerity, always ready to offer a helping hand (and a bit of cosmic confusion) to those who travel with him.

## Character Profile

Title: The Void-Drifter, Echo of the Weave

Archetype: Astral Sorcerer / Displaced Soul

Core Motivation: To find his way back to a sense of belonging and recover his lost relics from the physical world.

Combat Style: Erratic but powerful. His magic feels slightly unpredictable, as if it's still reacting to the rip in the Weave he passed through, allowing for sudden bursts of raw arcane energy.

## Game Description

The Void-Drifter, Echo of the Weave, is a displaced incarnation of Arcturus who brings unpredictable and powerful astral magic to the battlefield, seeking to find his lost relics among the stars.
