## Kira Valerius

## Backstory

Kira was once a high-ranking knight in the kingdom of Eldoria, situated on the western coast across the Great Sea. For centuries, Eldoria flourished as a beacon of art and chivalry until it fell victim to the Void-Blight—a creeping, necrotic corruption that drained color from the world and turned its heroes into hollow husks.

As her home crumbled under the weight of this spreading darkness, Kira led a small contingent of survivors across the churning waters to reach these new lands. Now, she wanders as a knight-errant, seeking out pockets of injustice to correct, hoping one day to gather enough strength and allies to return and reclaim Eldoria from the encroaching shadow.

## Character Profile

Title: The Exile Knight, Blade of the Shattered Shore

Archetype: Soldier / Versatile Warrior

Core Motivation: To strike down injustice wherever it appears, while carrying the heavy weight of a lost kingdom in her heart.

Combat Style: A balanced and reliable approach, utilizing steady defense and impactful strikes to manage both offense and protection.

## Game Description

A resilient knight with a steady heart, Kira embodies the essence of chivalry. Her dependable, all-around fighting style provides a perfect foundation for players looking to master the fundamentals of combat while enjoying a reliable warrior on the field.
