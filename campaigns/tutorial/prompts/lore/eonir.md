# Eonir the Still (The Lich)


## Visual Description

A towering Lich draped in heavy, deep violet robes that appear to fray into sharp, thorn-like spikes at every edge. His face is a skeletal visage with a permanent, subtle smirk, and his eyes glow with an icy white light. Most striking are his long, slender golden fingers, which seem to trace the ley lines of reality as he moves. He wears ornate vertical bands across his chest that pulse with a faint, rhythmic hum.

## Personality

Eonir is stoic, analytical, and possessed of a weary wisdom. Unlike many liches who are driven by an insatiable hunger for souls, Eonir is driven by a desire for *perspective*. He speaks in low, resonant tones, often pausing to observe the "geometry" of a room or the flow of time before he addresses others.

## Backstory

Once a mortal king and master scholar known as **Eonir Xavian Stalix**, he sought to "capture" the movement of the heavens into a single, permanent moment. In his obsession with freezing time, he fell into a magical slumber that eventually pulled his body into an undead state, turning him into the Sovereign of Stillness. He claimed Jyeshtha’s Relic because it is the only object capable of showing him what lies in the "Great Void" beyond his own kingdom—the places where existence hasn't yet been "still."

## Profile

Title: The Architect of Stillness

Name: Eonir the Still (Formerly Eonir Xavian Stalix)

Archetype: Lich / Sovereign of Stillness

Alignment: Neutral Evil

Key Connection: Current possessor of Jyeshtha’s Relic.

## Combat Style

Eonir rarely moves his body during a fight; instead, he manipulates the world around him. He uses **Gravity Manipulation** to pull enemies toward him or crush them into the earth, and **Shadowweaving** to create barriers of solidified darkness. When he finally strikes with his golden hands, it is usually a precise, reality-warping blow that can shatter both physical matter and magical shields alike.

## Dialogues

> *"Time is a chaotic river, traveler. My kingdom is the shore where I have commanded the waters to rest. But even in stillness... something awaits us."*
