# Mirantas Valerius Solari (Mira)

## Backstory

In a land perpetually threatened by creeping, suffocating shadows, Mira was born to a small village that served as a lonely outpost against the Great Gloom. While others feared the darkness, Mira found solace in the "Eternal Spark"—a flickering flame at the center of their local sanctuary. From a young age, she felt its warmth not just as heat, but as a physical presence of comfort and hope.

She joined the Order of the Resplendent Dawn as an acolyte, dedicating her life to understanding how light can both reveal truth and provide sanctuary. She spent years in silent prayer, learning to channel the radiance into her very being. However, she learned quickly that while healing is the greatest gift of the light, it often requires a courageous shield. When the shadows attacked their temple, Mira didn't just pray; she stepped forward, her staff glowing with intense brilliance to drive back the gloom and her sword ready to strike those who dared approach her wounded flock. She now travels as a wandering beacon, seeking to turn every shadow into a sanctuary and every wound into a story of recovery.

## Character Profile

Title: The Radiant Sentinel, Acolyte of the Eternal Spark

Archetype: Light Cleric / Holy Guardian

Core Motivation: To be a source of constant hope and healing in a world of shadows, protecting the weak and mending the broken.

Combat Style: Defensive support. Mira uses her staff to project "auras" of light that protect nearby allies, while weaving swift healing spells to mend wounds mid-battle. She wields her sword with steady purpose, moving into the fray only when her companions need a steadfast shield.
