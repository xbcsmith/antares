# Sirius Xylanthir

## Backstory

In the subterranean reaches where the light of the sun rarely touches the earth, Sirius rose to prominence not by lineage, but by sheer arcane willpower. As a Dark Elf scholar, he spent centuries mastering the "Void-Weave"—a form of sorcery that draws energy from the spaces between stars and the silence of the deep. 

Unlike his peers who sought to use magic for utility or beauty, Sirius viewed the arcane as a ladder of dominance. He became known as a relentless mage, one who would spend weeks perfecting a single spell just to ensure it could best a rival or overcome a daunting obstacle. His journey across the surface world is not a casual stroll; it is a calculated pursuit of untapped magical reservoirs and legendary artifacts. To Sirius, every enemy encountered is an opportunity to test his mastery, and every ally is either a useful tool or a potential student in his long-term study of the cosmos.

## Character Profile

Title: Master of the Obsidian Veil, Archon of the Void-Weave

Archetype: Shadow Sorcerer / Void Mage

Core Motivation: To achieve absolute mastery over the arcane arts and to command the flow of magic as a master commands an orchestra.

Combat Style: High-impact area control. Sirius favors heavy-hitting arcane blasts and shadow manipulation, using his staff to anchor powerful spells that can crush enemies or warp the environment around him.

## Game Description

Master of the Obsidian Veil, Archon of the Void-Weave, is a relentless elven mage who delights in using the arcane arts against others to achieve perfection and dominance.
