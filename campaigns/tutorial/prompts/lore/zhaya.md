# Zhaya Starbound

## Backstory

Raised among the high mountain monasteries of the Eastern Peaks, Zhaya belonged to an ancient order that sought to harmonize the body and spirit through rigorous discipline. While her peers found peace in the steady rhythm of meditation, Zhaya was haunted by visions—dreams of a celestial map called "the Astrologer's Path" that promised to reveal the ultimate truth of existence.

Her elders viewed these dreams as an obsession, perhaps even a source of fear, for they preferred the safety of known truths over the vast uncertainty of the cosmos. Driven by a desire to see what lay beyond the monastery walls, Zhaya eventually broke away from her teachers. She began a long pilgrimage following the stars east and south, eventually arriving at the Astronomers Temple, where the very stones are carved with the symbols of her dreams. Now, she seeks a sign that will finally lead her to the one true Astrologer—the being who reads fate as it is written, not merely as we wish it to be.

## Character Profile

Title: Celestial Monk, Seeker of the Star-Bound Truth

Archetype: Astral Monk / Fate-Reader

Core Motivation: To discover the one true Astrologer and decipher the celestial map that governs all existence.

Combat Style: Fluid, rhythmic martial arts that channel starlight into concentrated bursts of energy from her fists and movements, striking with both physical precision and spiritual grace.
